## How to use

1. Answer **closed-book**. Write your answer down *before* opening the explanation (click the `Answer` line to expand it).
2. For every miss, write a one-line explanation in your own words in `notes/mistakes.md`.
3. Re-do missed questions after 2 days and after 7 days.
4. Target **85%+** per domain before moving to full mock exams from external providers.
5. Real exam questions may be multiple-answer ("choose two") — these files mark that clearly.
